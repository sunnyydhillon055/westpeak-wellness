## What this comparison isolates

1. **34 points is the price of the off-site vacuum, net of everything.** Westpeak wins nine categories — more than both opponents combined — and loses the match. Category 5 alone (GBP, 50 vs 700–800) is a ~37-point swing on the overall; the phone (cat 18) and directories (cat 6) are each worth another 10–30. Any single owner decision from the off-site kit flips the overall ranking.
2. **Skylark is beatable on its own differentiator.** It employs a Punjabi-speaking counsellor and is invisible on every Punjabi search term checked — Westpeak already outranks it there (#7 on "punjabi counsellor abbotsford"). That advantage is Westpeak's only as long as Skylark keeps not noticing; the counter-move takes them one landing page.
3. **Bailey demonstrates what schema-with-an-address buys.** The weakest technical site of the three ranks #6 in its city, carried by premises, PostalAddress/Geo markup, consistent citations and a PT profile. It is a working proof of the exact stack Westpeak lacks.
4. **Both opponents publish; Westpeak's finished content sits in a drawer.** Cat 12 is the one content category Westpeak loses, and six publishable drafts are one clinical read away from flipping it.

*Skylark measured in-browser (it blocks scripted requests); Bailey scanned scripted; Westpeak from today's scan set. Scoring is editorial judgement against recorded evidence.*
