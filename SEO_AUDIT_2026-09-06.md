# Westpeak Wellness — Full SEO Audit

**Audited:** 6 September 2026, against commit `03b2b1f` (live at the time of writing)
**Scope:** every prerendered route in the production build (251 pages), the ten on-demand routes as served, the live host, Search Console exports held in `data/gsc/`, the public SERP landscape, and five competitors.
**Method:** programmatic parse of the built HTML (script: `C:\dev\wp-gates\crawl.mjs`), the repository's own 20 gates (all green on this build), live HTTP checks, Search Console CSVs, web search, and fetches of competitor pages. Every number below was read from something, and where a thing could not be measured it is marked as such rather than estimated.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph version

This is the best-built counselling site in its market and one of the least visible. Technically there is almost nothing to fix: titles, descriptions, canonicals, redirects, sitemap, robots, alt text, structured data and mobile all pass, and the gate suite enforces them on every deploy. Content is deep — median page is 1,019 words, 74 pages exceed 1,500 — and unique (the uniqueness gate holds the city × service set apart). Yet Search Console's last 28-day export shows **1,530 impressions and 1 click**, with the money terms sitting at **positions 40–85** ("online counselling vancouver" #43, "online counselling bc" #70, "emdr therapy online" #86). The gap is not on-page; it is authority and entity signals the site does not have — no Google Business Profile, no directory citations that link, a handful of social profiles and one BCACC register entry. Three things will move it: build the off-site footprint (§7), fix the two real on-site defects — language tagging and hreflang on the Punjabi and Tagalog surfaces (§4.1) — and push the eight pages already inside striking distance of page one (§3.2) over the line. The new counsellor changes the map: Tagalog and Alberta are the two least-contested spaces the practice can now legitimately claim.
