## What this audit changes

1. **The intersection bet has its first confirmed positions** (cat. 3). "punjabi counselling bc" #3/#5 and "punjabi counselling surrey" #7 on the Bing index are the first page-1 sightings in this project's history. The move: verify on Google via Search Console this week (filter queries containing "punjabi"), and put the Punjabi pages at the front of `SOCIAL_QUEUE.md`.
2. **The citation vacuum is now actively harmful** (cat. 7). Scrapers say White Rock; the registry scrape publishes a street address. This upgrades the off-site kit's NAP decision from "opportunity" to "correction": claiming the brand's citations is now defensive. ⚠️ Also a privacy question for the counsellor independent of marketing.
3. **The publishing-cadence score is voluntary** (cat. 14). Two seasonal pieces are finished and waiting on one clinical read. The calendar says early/late August; it is 28 August.
4. **Contact accessibility is the only conversion category lost** (cat. 22) — and it is lost 6/6. Every competitor answers a phone. The off-site kit's phone decision gates this *and* the GBP *and* half of category 7.
5. **Nothing in groups D and E is the constraint.** Westpeak is 1st in nine of ten on-site execution categories. The constraint is, as it was on 9 Aug and 27 Aug: demand, off-site, and the owner decisions in `OFFSITE_KIT_2026-08-27.md`.
