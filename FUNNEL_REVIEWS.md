# Funnel reviews — the monthly read that judges everything else

**Started:** 28 August 2026. Item 50 of `FIFTY_MORE_2026-08-28.md` — the discipline
that keeps the other forty-nine honest. Impressions are the input; **enquiries are
the verdict.** PATH_TO_950's closing rule applies: if the visibility number rises
and the calendar stays empty, the model is measuring the wrong thing.

## The monthly ritual (first Monday, ~20 minutes)

1. Open `/admin` — the funnel view and the inbound list.
2. Fill a row in the table below from what it shows. The `source` field on every
   enquiry records **which page earned it** — that column is the entire point.
3. Export fresh GSC pages + queries CSVs into `data/gsc/` (dated).
3b. Read the site-search log (`/admin` — fed by `/api/search-log`) for terms
    visitors searched and did not find. A zero-result query from a real visitor
    outranks any keyword tool: it is demand already on the site with nothing to
    land on. Add candidates to the month's decision.
4. One decision per month, written in the last column: what gets more effort, what
   gets retired, what gets tested. One. A review that produces five decisions
   produces zero.

## What to watch for, specifically

- **Pages that earn impressions but never enquiries** (the workplace cluster will
  likely look like this — informational traffic converts indirectly; judge it by
  assisted paths and brand searches, not last-click).
- **Pages that earn enquiries out of proportion to traffic** (the Punjabi surfaces
  are the candidates) — these get the next content investment.
- **The reply-time median** (surfaces on /contact at 5+ samples) — conversion work
  upstream is wasted if replies slow down.
- **Consult → client conversion** is Aman's number, not the site's. If consults
  book but clients do not start, that is a fit/fee/expectation conversation, and
  no amount of SEO touches it.
