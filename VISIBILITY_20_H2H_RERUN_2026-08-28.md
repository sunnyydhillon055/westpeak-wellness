**Scale:** /1,000 per category · **Rank:** position among the three

## Read this first: what a same-day re-run can and cannot show

The first pass ran earlier today. Nothing off-site can meaningfully have moved in
the hours between — no Google Business Profile appeared, no directory profile was
created, no citation was corrected. **A re-run this soon measures the instrument,
not the world**, and it is being reported that way.

Two things did change, and only one of them is the world:

1. **The world:** Darcy Bailey moved from #6 to #5 on `counselling langley`, and
   Westpeak's own sitemap grew from 177 to 192 URLs (today's shipping, including
   `/refer/doctor`).
2. **The instrument:** the first pass scored Skylark **680 on publishing cadence**
   and described both opponents as running "real dated blogs". Skylark's post
   sitemap says otherwise — 15 posts, the newest dated **1 July 2024**. The blog
   is not a cadence; it is an archive that stopped 26 months ago. That was a
   measurement error in the first pass, and correcting it moves the overall
   standings more than anything either practice actually did.

That correction is the headline. The rest is confirmation.

## What the re-run changes

1. **Skylark is not the content threat the first pass said it was.** Its blog stopped
   in July 2024. It ranks #6 in its own city on premises, phone, directories and a
   28-year-old brand — not on publishing. Any practice that publishes consistently
   walks past it on content over the next year; Westpeak has nine such pieces
   written and waiting.
2. **Bailey is now the site to beat, and it is beatable on craft.** It leads on the
   two things Westpeak cannot fix from a keyboard (premises, cadence) while shipping
   zero H1s on its homepage, a 270 kB front page, no forms anywhere, and no AI
   surface. It is a working demonstration that the off-site stack outvotes the
   on-site stack.
3. **Seven points, not thirty-four.** The first pass's headline — "the gap is
   smaller than any single owner decision" — is now emphatically true. Category 5
   alone (50 vs 800) is a ~37-point swing. **A Google Business Profile, on its own,
   makes Westpeak first.**
4. **Two categories moved on Westpeak's own work today** — indexable coverage to
   first at 192 URLs, and publishing cadence off the floor — and were cancelled out
   by a fifth scraper found on the brand SERP. The off-site vacuum is not static;
   left alone it fills with other people's wrong data.
