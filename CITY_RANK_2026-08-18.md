### The sharper test, and what it isolates

Absence from a competitive SERP has an innocent explanation: a young domain
loses to established ones. So I ran the same city terms **restricted to
westpeakwellness.com**, which removes every competitor. Then I ran exact
phrases that exist nowhere else on the internet — `"103 clients across 519
appointments"` from the Prince George pages, `"128,305"` from the Surrey
Punjabi page.

**Not one city page came back.** The index returned the same three URLs every
time:

- `/`
- `/services/depression-counselling`
- `/guides/waiting-for-therapy-in-bc`

All twelve city URLs are present and correct in `sitemap.xml`. All ten render
at 200 with full content — I fetched `/online-counselling/kelowna` directly and
got the title, ~1,200 words and fourteen in-body internal links exactly as
built.

So this is not a ranking problem. **The city pages are not retrievable**, and
that is a different failure with a different fix. Writing them better cannot
help a page that is not in the index.

> **Caveat, stated plainly.** The search tool available here is not Google. Its
> index of a small domain can lag. This is strong evidence of thin indexation
> on at least one major engine, not proof of absence from Google. **The one
> check that settles it is Search Console → Pages → Indexed.** Until somebody
> looks at that, everything above is an inference from one index.

## The comparison that should sting

**Clearheart Counselling appeared in four of the six city SERPs** — Abbotsford,
Victoria, Kelowna and Prince George — with `/virtual-locations-bc/<city>/`.
Templated pages, roughly 29 of them.

Westpeak's pages are better. They are longer, sourced, and argue something
specific and true about each city. They appeared in none.

`lib/locations.ts` already predicted this, in its own amendment dated 13 August:

> *"Templated city pages do not inherently lose. They lose WHEN THE DOMAIN HAS
> NO AUTHORITY TO PUSH THEM — which was, and still is, the situation here."*

That was a hypothesis. It is now measured. Six cities of hand-written, cited,
genuinely local content lost to a template, and the reason was never the
writing.

## Structural findings

Real defects, found by crawling the built HTML rather than the source.

**1. `/punjabi-counselling` has no hub page** — but the cluster is not orphaned,
and my first pass said it was. `app/punjabi-counselling/` contains only
`[region]`, so there is no index at that URL the way `/online-counselling` has
one. The Gurmukhi page at `/punjabi` does link all four regions; it is rendered
on demand rather than prerendered, so it leaves no static file and my crawl of
the build could not see it. `npm run seo` prints that warning explicitly, which
is how the error was caught, and the script now supplements the crawl with a
source scan of on-demand routes.

Corrected, the gap is smaller but real: Punjabi pages average **4.25 in-body
inbound links**, the city pages **7.5**. Kamloops moved from 5th to 4th once the
missing link was counted.

**2. Neither the homepage nor `/services` links to any city page.** Zero, from
both. Every path to a city page runs through one hub or a sibling.

**3. Abbotsford and Victoria have zero in-body inbound links from outside their
own cluster.** They are linked by their five siblings and the hub, and by
nothing else on the site. Compare Prince George, which is linked from
`/for/rotational-and-camp-workers`, two guides and two service pages — and
which tops this ranking largely because of it.

**4. Abbotsford has no Punjabi page.** `lib/locations.ts` says Abbotsford has
*"one of the largest Punjabi-speaking communities in Canada, with much the same
dynamic as Surrey"*. `lib/punjabi-regions.ts` names Abbotsford as one of the
three places where BC's Punjabi-speaking counsellors are. A competitor ranks on
the phrase "Punjabi counselling in Surrey and Abbotsford". The site's own two
files argue for this page and it does not exist.

**5. Vancouver has no Punjabi page either** — also named in
`punjabi-regions.ts` as one of the three centres.

**6. Kamloops has no `/online-counselling` page.** It is the only city in the
set present in one cluster and not the other, which is most of why a genuinely
strong page ranks fifth.

**7. The Punjabi cluster is better evidenced than the city cluster.** Every one
of the four Punjabi pages rests on a citable census figure. Of the six
online-counselling pages, **one** does — Prince George. The other five argue
from fair characterisation: Vancouver's cost of living, the Valley's distances,
the ferry, the Okanagan's wildfire seasons. All true, none checkable, and it is
the single largest recoverable gap in the Page band.

## What would actually move these numbers

In order of points per unit of effort, which is not the order of appeal.

**Off-site, and it needs a person rather than a terminal.** Psychology Today,
Counselling BC and First Session appeared in *every one* of the ten SERPs above.
Directory profiles are the mechanism by which a new counselling practice becomes
visible in a city it has no office in, and the practice is in none of them. That
is 90 of the 146 forgone off-site points per city, in seven cities at once.

Note what Surrey's extra 30 points are: the homepage `<title>` reads
"… | Surrey", the BCACC register entry is Surrey-based, and the TikTok account
resolves to Surrey. Not one of those was an SEO decision. It is the only city
the wider web associates with this practice at all, and it happened by accident
— which is a fair illustration of how little deliberate off-site work exists.

**Confirm indexation before anything else.** Search Console → Pages. If the city
pages show as "Discovered – currently not indexed" or "Crawled – currently not
indexed", the finding above is confirmed and the fix is internal links and
authority, not content. If they are indexed, then this is an ordinary ranking
problem and the answer is different. **These two diagnoses lead opposite ways,
and one look settles which.**

**On-site, cheap, and worth doing regardless** — each of these is a real defect
found above, not a suggestion:

- link city pages from the homepage and `/services` (currently zero)
- build the `/punjabi-counselling` hub
- give Abbotsford and Victoria inbound links from guides and audience pages
- add the Abbotsford and Vancouver Punjabi pages the repo's own files argue for
- add `/online-counselling/kamloops`
- find one citable figure for each of the five unsourced city pages

Together those are worth roughly 60–90 points per city. Set against 350 for
search presence and 146 for off-site, that is the honest proportion — and the
reason this ranking puts the first column first.

## Method

`scripts/city-rank.mjs`. Structural inputs come from `.next/server/app/**` — the
rendered HTML, not the source — with `<script>` blocks stripped first, because
Next serialises the RSC flight payload into every page and a raw grep of one
page finds text from all the others. Only `<main>` is counted, because nav and
footer links appear on all 116 pages and counting them makes every page look
equally well linked.

SERP and off-site inputs are a dated block at the top of the script, because
they decay and because pretending they are live would be worse than admitting
they are a snapshot.

**Four defects in this script, all found and fixed before the numbers above.**
The schema check matched only scalar `"@type"` and missed the array form
`["MedicalBusiness","ProfessionalService"]`, so it scored every page as having
no local-business markup. The sitemap check looked at `app/sitemap.ts` when the
sitemap is a route handler at `app/sitemap.xml/route.ts`. The sourcing term was
a no-op that silently duplicated the hard-figure term. And the crawl ignored
on-demand routes, which undercounted every Punjabi page by one inbound link and
produced a wrong finding — that the Punjabi cluster had no hub at all — until
`npm run seo` printed the warning that contradicted it.

The first defect showed up as a suspiciously uniform 40/70 technical score
across all seven cities. Uniformity is usually a bug, not a finding.
