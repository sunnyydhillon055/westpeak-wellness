# Keywords, visibility, client acquisition and AI crawlability — 25 categories, six practices

**Measured:** 28 August 2026, late evening · **Scale:** /1,000 per category, overall is the mean
**New comparator set**, chosen by measurement rather than assumption — see below.

## Why this set, and why the result differs from tonight's other audit

The head-to-head run earlier tonight scored Westpeak **550, third of three**, against Skylark
and Darcy Bailey. This one scores it **636, first of six**. Both are correct, and anyone
quoting one without the other is misrepresenting the position.

Two things changed:

1. **The opponents.** Skylark and Bailey were picked as *local office incumbents* — the
   practices Westpeak loses to in Abbotsford and Langley on the strength of premises,
   phones and directory profiles. This set was picked by **measured appearance across
   twelve commercial queries** run tonight, which surfaces the practices actually visible
   to a BC-wide searcher. Two of the five turn out to be Squarespace sites that are
   technically weak.
2. **The scale.** Twenty-five categories weighted toward keywords, machine readability and
   client acquisition — the three things asked for. That is a scale Westpeak is built to
   win. The twenty-category scale weighted off-site presence more heavily, and Westpeak
   loses that one.

**Neither number is a client.** Westpeak takes first place here while finishing **last in
all four off-site categories** and **last on head-term footprint** — the categories that
determine whether a stranger with a problem ever reaches the site.

**1 · Head-term footprint.** Across ten general commercial queries — `online counselling bc`,
`couples counselling bc`, `emdr therapy bc`, `anxiety counselling british columbia`,
`trauma therapy bc`, `virtual counselling british columbia`, `counselling surrey bc`,
`counselling abbotsford`, `counselling langley`, `marriage counselling fraser valley` —
Westpeak appears in **zero** top-ten results. Crossroads and Wellspring appear three times
each. This is the category that matters most and the one Westpeak is worst at.

**2 · Niche-term ownership.** All three of Westpeak's top-ten appearances are Punjabi terms:
`punjabi counsellor abbotsford` #7, `punjabi counselling bc` #4 and #5. **No competitor in
this set appears on any Punjabi term.** The moat is real, undefended by anyone else, and
currently the only reason a stranger finds this site at all.

**5 · Brand SERP.** Westpeak owns results #1, #2, #8, #9 and #10 for its own name — but #3

only practice in the set whose non-owned brand results are *actively wrong about it*.
Thrive has more intruders but they are unrelated businesses sharing a common word;
Wellspring controls eight of ten.

**The market is dispersed.** No practice appears in more than three of twelve queries. There
is no incumbent to displace — there is a vacuum, and directories (Psychology Today,
counsellingbc.com) are taking the top slots that no practice has claimed.

**12 · The AI policy distinction that matters.** Three states, not two:

- **Westpeak: an authored policy.** Eleven AI agents — GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User,
  ClaudeBot, Claude-User, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Google-Extended,
  Bingbot, Applebot-Extended — each in its **own group** with `Allow: /`. A decision.
- **Being and Becoming, Thrive: platform boilerplate.** Twelve AI agents named, but every
  one of them listed consecutively *with* `User-agent: *`, so per the robots specification
  they share the catch-all group and get Squarespace's ordinary narrow disallows. Naming
  without a rule of their own is not a policy; it is a default they did not choose.
- **Wellspring, Wellbeings, Crossroads: silent.** Default-allow, no AI statement.

**13 · The llms.txt moat is narrowing.** Earlier audits in this repo recorded Westpeak as the
only site in its market with an `llms.txt`. That is no longer true: **Wellspring and
Crossroads both serve one**, Wellspring's generated automatically by the Rank Math SEO
plugin. Westpeak still leads — it is the only one with `llms-full.txt`, and its file is
hand-written rather than templated — but the differentiator is being commoditised by
plugins and should be expected to disappear as a *distinguishing* asset within a year.

**16 · No-JS content.** All six return substantial text without JavaScript. Ratio of words
to bytes is the useful measure: Westpeak returns 1,633 words in 93 kB; Thrive returns 1,590
words in 376 kB. An extraction crawler on a budget gets four times the substance per
request from Westpeak.

**14 · Structured data.** Types observed: Westpeak 26 (validator-gated, zero errors), Being
and Becoming 21, Crossroads 18, Wellspring 17, Wellbeings 15, Thrive 8. Westpeak is the only
one carrying `MedicalWebPage` *and* `FAQPage` *and* `SpeakableSpecification`. It is also the
only one with **no** `PostalAddress` — deliberate, and the subject of category 19.

## What this audit says to do

1. **Head-term footprint is the constraint, and content is not what is missing.** Zero
   top-ten appearances across ten commercial queries, against a site with better craft,
   better targeting and better machine-readability than any competitor measured. The
   missing inputs are the off-site four — profile, directories, citations, and a phone that
   makes a consistent NAP possible.
2. **The market is a vacuum, not a fortress.** No practice appears in more than three of
   twelve queries; directories hold the slots practices have not claimed. This is unusual
   and it favours whoever builds off-site presence first.
3. **Defend the Punjabi niche and extend it.** It is the only place Westpeak ranks, no
   competitor in either audit set contests it, and the fluent-review constraint is the only
   thing rate-limiting it.
4. **Bank the AI lead now, because it is depreciating.** Rank Math already ships `llms.txt`
   automatically; Squarespace already ships AI-agent boilerplate. Speakable markup, an
   authored robots policy and `llms-full.txt` still separate Westpeak, but the gap closes on
   its own as platforms catch up. The advantage is worth most in the next twelve months.
5. **Nobody in this market captures enquiries.** Five of five competitors run zero forms.
   Every visitor who is not ready to book is lost by all of them. Westpeak already has the
   mechanism; what it lacks is the traffic to put through it — which is point 1 again.

**Method.** Six sites scanned scripted — robots.txt, full sitemap crawl, and a six-page
sitemap-stride sample each — with one request at a time and delays between. Search positions
from DuckDuckGo (`kl=ca-en`), DuckDuckGo's own modules excluded from position counting,
across twelve queries. Competitor Search Console data is unknowable and no category depends
on it. Scoring is editorial judgement against the recorded evidence.

**Two instrument errors were found and fixed before scoring**, and are recorded here because
this repo's standing lesson is to verify the instrument before acting on what it reports:

1. The AI-bot detector built its pattern by string concatenation and lost a backslash,
   producing `user-agent:s*GPTBot` — a pattern that matches nothing. It reported that **no
   site in the set names any AI crawler**, including the one whose robots.txt names eleven.
2. The "blocks AI crawlers" test looked for any `Disallow:` beginning with `/` near an agent
   line, and so counted `Disallow: /*/opengraph-image` as a site-wide block. It reported
   that **Westpeak blocks every AI crawler** — the exact opposite of the truth.

Both were caught because the output contradicted a known fact about the site under test.
Neither would have been caught by looking at the competitors alone, which is an argument for
always including a site you know intimately in any comparison set.
