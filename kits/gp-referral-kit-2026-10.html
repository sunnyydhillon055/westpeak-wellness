How a patient gets here: no referral needed

Counselling with a Registered Clinical Counsellor is not an MSP service, so there is no referral form and no letter to write. The patient books a free 15-minute consultation themselves at westpeakwellness.com/book, which shows the real open times. The consultation is where fit and scope are settled, and where a counsellor will say plainly if another service would suit the patient better.

To ask about scope or fit before suggesting us, email info@westpeakwellness.com. With the patient’s written consent, information can be shared with the clinic; without it, nothing about a patient can be confirmed.

Weekly EMDR is billed as an individual session. Many extended health plans reimburse an RCC, depending on the plan; patients check theirs. No MSP billing and no direct billing. After a crash, ICBC pre-approves 12 counselling sessions in the first 12 weeks with no referral; this practice is not in ICBC’s Recovery Network, so the patient pays and claims.

For a patient on a medical leave

The free stress leave pay calculator at westpeakwellness.com/tools/stress-leave-pay-calculator shows a patient, week by week, what BC sick days and EI sickness benefits would pay on their own wage, and where a disability plan or WorkSafeBC takes over. Only a physician or nurse practitioner can certify the leave itself; a counsellor cannot.

Scope limits

  • No diagnosis, prescribing or formal psychological assessment.
  • No court-ordered, custody or forensic work.
  • Not a crisis service: sessions are scheduled, with no on-call line.
  • Active psychosis, eating disorders needing medical monitoring, or withdrawal management are better held by a specialised team.
In crisis: 9-1-1 if in immediate danger · 9-8-8, call or text · BC Mental Health Support Line 310-6789, no area code needed.
