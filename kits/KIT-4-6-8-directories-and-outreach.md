## Bing Places

[bingplaces.com](https://www.bingplaces.com/) → **Import from Google Business
Profile.** Do this *after* Kit 1 is verified and it takes about three minutes,
because it copies everything across. Doing it before means entering it all
twice.

## Apple Business Connect

[businessconnect.apple.com](https://businessconnect.apple.com/) — free, and it
is what Apple Maps and Siri read. Register as a **service-area business**, same
fields as Kit 1. Under-used by small practices, which is precisely why it is
worth twenty minutes.

Template — **stay under 200 words and never reference a client**:

```
Aman Bains Dhillon, MA, RCC — Registered Clinical Counsellor, Westpeak Wellness

[Two or three tight paragraphs answering exactly what was asked. Speak from
```

### (d) Local news pitch

Subject: **Punjabi-language therapy access in the Lower Mainland**

```
Hello,

There is a story here that I do not think has been covered locally: BC has a
large Punjabi-speaking population and very few counsellors who can work in
Punjabi. The practical effect is that people either wait, go without, or do
therapy in their second language — which is harder than it sounds, because the
vocabulary for grief, shame and family obligation often only exists properly in
the first one.

I am a Registered Clinical Counsellor offering sessions in Punjabi across BC,
and my Master's research was on intergenerational trauma in the South Asian
community. I am happy to speak to the access gap, why first-language therapy
changes what is possible in a session, and what the stigma actually looks like
generationally — without discussing any individual.

Aman Bains Dhillon, MA, RCC
Westpeak Wellness · https://www.westpeakwellness.com
```

**Send to:** Surrey Now-Leader, Abbotsford News, Vancouver Sun health desk,
CBC British Columbia, Link Newspaper, Voice Newspaper.

# Kit 8 — Brand defence

## The confusion

"Peak Wellness Therapy" and similar names exist. Two things protect the brand:

1. **Never vary the name.** It is `Westpeak Wellness` — one word, then one
   word — everywhere, forever. Kit 2 exists because one listing already got
   this wrong.
2. **Own the brand query.** Once Kit 1 is verified, a search for "Westpeak
   Wellness" returns the GBP panel, the site, and the directory profiles. That
   crowds out the confusion without a word of comparison.

## Monitoring

Set up [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts) for:

```
"Westpeak Wellness"
"West Peak Wellness"
"Aman Bains Dhillon"
```

The middle one is the useful alert — it catches anyone citing the practice
under the wrong name, which is the citation inconsistency that costs the most.

## Responding to an unsolicited review

If a Google review appears, **do not confirm the person was a client.** Doing
so discloses a therapeutic relationship, which is a confidentiality breach
regardless of what they said publicly first.

**Positive review — safe reply:**

```
Thank you for taking the time to leave this. I am glad the site and the process
were helpful.
```

**Negative review — safe reply:**

```
Thank you for the feedback. I take concerns seriously and would welcome the
chance to discuss this directly — info@westpeakwellness.com. For anyone who
would prefer an independent route, the BC Association of Clinical Counsellors
has a formal complaints process at bcacc.ca.
```

Neither reply confirms or denies anyone was a client. **Never** write "I'm
sorry your sessions didn't help" or anything else that concedes the
relationship. If a review is defamatory or names clinical detail, report it to
Google for removal and document it — do not argue in public.

**And never solicit a review, from anyone, ever.**
