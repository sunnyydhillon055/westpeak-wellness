Scope — what this practice does not do

  • No diagnosis. An RCC does not diagnose mental disorders. A formal diagnosis for a disability claim, an accommodation or a medication decision needs a physician, psychiatrist or registered psychologist.
  • No prescribing. Where medication is part of the picture the work runs alongside the prescriber, not instead.
  • No formal psychological assessment. Psychoeducational, ADHD, autism and cognitive testing all require a registered psychologist.
  • No court-ordered or forensic work. No custody evaluations, parenting assessments, or anything intended as evidence.
  • Not a crisis service. Sessions are scheduled and there is no on-call line.
  • Not a fit for every presentation. Active psychosis, an eating disorder needing medical monitoring, or substance dependence needing withdrawal management are better held by a specialised team. Saying so at the consultation is a normal outcome, not a rejection — and the referral back to you will be explicit.
In crisis: 9-1-1 if in immediate danger · 9-8-8 call or text, any hour · BC Mental Health Support 310-6789, no area code needed.

How to refer

No referral form, no letter, and no referral is required — counselling is not a referred service in BC. The patient books the free 15-minute consultation themselves at westpeakwellness.com/book, which is also where they can see real availability before committing to anything.

If it would help to speak first — scope, fit, or whether the wait is realistic for someone — email info@westpeakwellness.com. Replies within one business day. With the patient's written consent, information can be shared both ways; without it, nothing about a patient can be confirmed, including whether they attended.
