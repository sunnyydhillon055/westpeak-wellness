# Kit 2 — Correct the BCACC listing

**Time: 15 minutes. Do this second.**

An incorrect citation is worse than a missing one. The BCACC register entry
reportedly reads **"West Peak Wellness"** with Surrey / in-person framing. Every
part of that contradicts the website: the practice name is one word, and it is
fully virtual serving all of BC.

BCACC is also the highest-authority citation available here — it is the
regulator's own public register, it is what a careful client checks before
booking, and the site links to it from `/about`, `/standards` and
`/resources/verify-a-counsellor-in-bc`. A visitor who follows that link and
finds a different practice name has been given a reason to doubt.

---

## Where to go

[bcacc.ca](https://bcacc.ca/) → **Member Login** (top right) → **My Profile** /
**Edit Directory Listing**.

If the login is not to hand, use *Forgot password* with the address BCACC has
on file. If the listing is not editable from the member area, email
`info@bcacc.ca` from the registered address with the subject **"Directory
listing correction — [your registration number]"** and paste the field table
below into the message.

---

## Field-by-field

| BCACC field | Set it to |
|---|---|
| Practice / business name | `Westpeak Wellness` |
| Website | `https://www.westpeakwellness.com` |
| Email | `info@westpeakwellness.com` |
| City | `Surrey` *(see note below)* |
| Delivery method | **Online / virtual only** — untick any in-person option |
| Areas served | `All of British Columbia (virtual)` |
| Languages | `English`, `Punjabi` |
| Accepting new clients | **Yes** |
| Session fee | `$140` individual · `$170` couples |
| Phone | Leave blank if permitted — see the README |

> **On the City field.** Most registers require one and use it for geographic
> filtering. Keep the real city of registration; do **not** change it to
> Vancouver to chase traffic. What must change is the *framing* — the profile
> text below makes clear that sessions are virtual and province-wide, so the
> city is where the counsellor is registered rather than where a client would
> travel to.

## Profile text — paste as-is

**Short description (if there is a character limit around 200):**

```
Westpeak Wellness is a fully virtual counselling practice serving clients
throughout British Columbia. Individual and couples therapy in English and
Punjabi, including EMDR, trauma, anxiety and depression work.
```

**Full profile / about:**

```
Westpeak Wellness is a virtual counselling practice serving clients throughout
British Columbia by secure video. Sessions are available in English and
Punjabi.

The practice offers individual counselling for anxiety, depression, trauma and
burnout, and Gottman-informed couples therapy. EMDR is available for trauma and
for memories that continue to intrude despite what you understand
intellectually. Work is trauma-informed and paced deliberately — stabilisation
before processing, and a plan that is reviewed rather than assumed.

Cultural context is a particular focus. The counsellor's Master's research
examined intergenerational trauma in the South Asian community, and sessions
can be conducted in Punjabi, English, or a mix of both. Family expectation,
obligation and generational silence around mental health do not need explaining
from scratch.

Because the practice is entirely online, there is no commute and no waiting
room, and clients anywhere in the province — including the North and the
Interior — have the same access as those in the Lower Mainland.

New clients are welcome. A free 15-minute consultation is available to check
fit before booking a session: https://www.westpeakwellness.com/book
```

**Specialties — tick or enter these:**

```
Anxiety · Depression · Trauma / PTSD · EMDR · Couples counselling ·
Relationship issues · Burnout and work stress · Cultural identity ·
Intergenerational trauma · Grief and loss · Self-esteem · Life transitions
```

**Modalities:**

```
EMDR · Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) · Gottman Method ·
Emotion-Focused Therapy · Internal Family Systems (informed) ·
Somatic (informed) · Mindfulness-based approaches · Narrative Therapy
```

## Before you save — three checks

1. Practice name reads **Westpeak Wellness**, one word then one word.
2. The URL has **`https://www.`** on the front. Not the bare domain.
3. Nothing anywhere implies an office, a street address, or in-person sessions.
