# Kit 1 — Google Business Profile

**Time: 45 minutes, then ~10 days waiting for the verification postcard or
video call.** Worth more than everything else in this folder combined.

---

## Before you start: the one decision that matters

GBP will ask whether you serve customers at your address. **Answer no.** Then
it becomes a **service-area business**: your address is collected for
verification and then **hidden from the public listing**, and you appear for
searches across the service area you define rather than at a pin.

Entering an address as a visitable location for a virtual practice creates a
listing that contradicts the website, invites "permanently closed" edits from
strangers who find nothing there, and risks suspension. Say no.

---

## Step 1 — Claim

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com/) and sign in with
   the account that should own this long-term. **Use a Google account you
   control permanently** — moving ownership later is a support ticket, not a
   setting.
2. Search **Westpeak Wellness**. If a listing already exists, claim it. If not,
   create it.
3. When asked *"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"* → **No**.

## Step 3 — Description (750 characters, paste as-is)

```
Westpeak Wellness is a virtual counselling practice serving clients throughout
British Columbia by secure video, in English and Punjabi.

Individual counselling covers anxiety, depression, trauma, burnout and life
transitions. Couples work is Gottman-informed and begins with assessment rather
than advice. EMDR is available for trauma and for memories that keep intruding
regardless of what you understand intellectually.

Cultural context is a particular focus — family expectation, obligation and
generational silence do not need explaining from scratch.

Sessions are online, so clients anywhere in the province have the same access.
Registered Clinical Counsellor (RCC), BC Association of Clinical Counsellors.

A free 15-minute consultation is available before booking.
```

## Step 5 — Attributes

## Step 6 — Photos

GBP listings with photos get materially more engagement. **Do not upload stock
images** — Google's guidelines ask for genuine photos, and stock is
identifiable.

Shot list, in priority order:

1. **Logo** — export `public/img/logo.svg` at 512×512 PNG.
2. **Cover** — a calm BC landscape you photographed yourself (the site already
   uses Fairy Lake and a forest path; anything in that register works).
3. **Professional headshot** — the same one as `/about`, so the two match.
4. **The workspace as clients see it** — the actual video-call background.
   This one is genuinely useful: it is what a client will look at for 50
   minutes, and showing it lowers the barrier to a first session.
5. 2–3 more BC landscapes for rotation.

Captions can be plain: *"Westpeak Wellness — online counselling across British
Columbia."*

## Step 7 — Seed the Q&A (10 minutes, high value)

You can ask and answer your own questions on your listing, and Google displays
them. This is the fastest legitimate way to get real answers onto the profile.
Post each question from a **second** Google account, then answer from the
business account.

1. **Do you offer sessions in Punjabi?**
   Yes — sessions run in Punjabi, English, or a mix of both. There is also a
   page written in Punjabi at westpeakwellness.com/punjabi.

2. **Do I need a doctor's referral?**
   No. You can book directly, including the free 15-minute consultation.

3. **How much does a session cost?**
   $140 for a 50-minute individual session and $170 for couples. Fees are
   listed at westpeakwellness.com/pricing.

4. **Is counselling covered by MSP?**
   Counselling with an RCC is not covered by MSP, but most extended health
   plans reimburse RCC sessions. A receipt suitable for claiming is issued
   after every session.

5. **Are sessions in person or online?**
   All sessions are online by secure video, anywhere in British Columbia.

6. **What is EMDR?**
   A structured therapy for trauma and intrusive memories. There is a full
   explanation at westpeakwellness.com/services/emdr-therapy.

7. **How do I know if we are a good fit?**
   Start with the free 15-minute consultation — it exists for exactly that,
   and there is no obligation to book afterwards.

8. **Do you work with couples?**
   Yes, using the Gottman Method. Couples sessions are 50 minutes at $170,
   with a 110-minute extended option.

9. **What areas of BC do you serve?**
   All of them. Because sessions are online there is no difference in access
   between Vancouver, Prince George or anywhere else in the province.

10. **What happens in a first session?**
    Mostly you talking and the counsellor asking questions, to understand the
    shape of things before working on them. A full walkthrough is at
    westpeakwellness.com/guides/what-to-expect-first-therapy-session.

## Step 9 — Once verified, send me the URL

Paste me the profile URL and I will set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_URL`,
which adds it to the site's `sameAs` — the link that lets a search engine
confirm the listing and the website are the same entity. That connection is
worth points on both sides and the slot is already wired and waiting.

## What not to do

- **Never request reviews from counselling clients**, including former ones.
  BCACC prohibits it. GBP will prompt you to "ask for reviews" — ignore it.
  Unsolicited reviews that arrive on their own are fine to receive; see Kit 8
  for how to respond without confirming anyone was a client.
- Do not add keywords to the business name.
- Do not add a visitable address.
