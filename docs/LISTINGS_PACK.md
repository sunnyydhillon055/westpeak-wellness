## Checked 3 Oct 2026, not eligible or paid

Listed so nobody researches them again. Each was read on its own site that
day; re-read before acting if a year has passed.

- **bc211** — its inclusion criteria (bc.211.ca/listing-inclusion-criteria,
  read 3 Oct 2026) admit non-profit, community and government services and
  businesses licensed or contracted by government to provide free or
  low-cost services. A fee-for-service private practice is none of those.
- **HealthLink BC directory** — provincial, health-authority and non-profit
  services only.
- **Healing in Colour** — listing needs a paid membership.
- **Inclusive Therapists** — paid; the free sponsored tier is for Indigenous
  and Black providers.
- **South Asian Therapists** (southasiantherapists.org) — £19.59 a month;
  only registered charities are listed free.
- **Counselling BC** — a month free, then an annual fee. Kept as item 4 for
  the owner's decision, because AI answers cite it.
- **Theravive** — $10 a month, or free in exchange for linking to Theravive.
  That is a link exchange; do not do it.
- **First Session** — $500 onboarding plus 25% commission.
- **TherapyDen paid tier** — the free profile does not appear in TherapyDen's
  own search results, so it is a link page only; the paid tier is not taken.
