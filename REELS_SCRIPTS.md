## 4. Therapy in Punjabi or in English: does the language matter?

**HOOK (on screen, 2s):** Therapy in Punjabi or in English: does the language matter?

**BEAT 1:** For many bilingual people, feelings and family live in the first language and work and analysis live in the second.

**BEAT 2:** Therapy in Punjabi can reach childhood memory, family relationships and emotion with words that were actually there when those things happened — including the words that have no English equivalent.

**BEAT 3:** Therapy in English can offer useful distance from exactly the same material, which is sometimes what makes it speakable at all.

**BEAT 4:** Neither is the correct choice in general; the good news is that this is not a decision you have to get right in advance, because a bilingual counsellor lets the session move between the two.

## 18. EMDR intensives vs weekly EMDR: which format fits?

**HOOK (on screen, 2s):** EMDR intensives vs weekly EMDR: which format fits?

**BEAT 1:** Weekly EMDR spreads the eight phases across regular 50-minute sessions, with the reprocessing itself often occupying a fraction of each one.

**BEAT 2:** An intensive books a longer block — here, 90 minutes — so that preparation, processing and closing happen inside a single arc instead of being cut to fit the hour.

**BEAT 3:** Intensives suit people with a specific target memory, stable day-to-day footing, and a schedule that makes weekly attendance hard.

**BEAT 4:** Weekly pacing remains the better choice when stabilisation is still the main work, which is common with complex or long-standing trauma.

## 19. Gottman Method vs EFT: two roads into couples therapy

**HOOK (on screen, 2s):** Gottman Method vs EFT: two roads into couples therapy

**BEAT 1:** The Gottman Method starts from the outside in: a structured assessment of how the two of you actually interact, then specific interventions aimed at the patterns — criticism, defensiveness, contempt, stonewalling — that its research links to relationships failing.

**BEAT 2:** Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) starts from the inside out: it treats the argument as the surface of an attachment question ("are you there for me?") and works on the emotional cycle underneath before the behaviour.

**BEAT 3:** Many couples would be helped by either; the honest differentiators are what you want to work on first and which style of session you can imagine yourselves in.

**CLOSE (on screen):** https://www.westpeakwellness.com/compare/gottman-method-vs-eft-for-couples
